01

Method

No eyeballing. Every metric comes from a specific signal in the server response, and the whole run reproduces with one command on a clean machine.

nametitle0%
priceprice0%
priceCurrencycurrency0%
brandbrand0%
skuSKU0%
availabilityavailability0%
gtinGTIN/EAN/UPC — see caveat0%

Every field is looked up by an exact, checkable rule: the name comes from the product entity, price, currency and availability from its offers — they often sit in a separate Offer node with no name of its own. gtin is counted strictly: a gtin/ gtinN field, or an identifier whose name contains gtin/ean/upc.

02

What it means

The storefront tells the agent what the product is and what it costs — and stays silent on which SKU it is and whether it is in stock. Without availability, stock cannot be checked before the charge; without a SKU, the item cannot be matched to a substitute or to anything else.

!

Availability — 2,7%. The most damaging of the three gaps: agentic commerce protocols require a stock check immediately before the charge. With availability missing, checkout should never reach payment.

!

SKU — 2,7%. Product identifiers are almost entirely absent, so an agent can neither match an item nor substitute an unavailable one nor assemble a cart from several variants.

i

Caveat on GTIN. gtin is 0% in the main set and in the control group. Either GTIN is genuinely not published in JSON-LD, or our definition is too strict. That is why GTIN is not used as an argument: the comparison says nothing when both values are zero. The arguments rest on sku and availability, where the gap is unambiguous.

i

Independent corroboration. Gartner: roughly 40% of AI assistants skip products with incomplete structured data, and only 23% of stores carry full Product markup. Structured feeds supply 34% of all agent candidates and 61% of shortlist entries.

i

And this is already money. AI referral traffic to retailers grew 393% year over year; Shopify reports ×7 traffic and ×11 orders via AI; and customers arriving from AI traffic convert 42% better than from ordinary search. The channel is growing — and the products in it are invisible.

Six defects the control group found

Each of them changed the numbers. That is why the fix history is published alongside the result: a report built without a control group very likely contains false figures — and any technical reader will spot them before the author does.

Image links were counted as product cards
.webp and .jpg hrefs landed in the list. Control: Samsung RU came out as unreachable.
fixed: filter by extension
Sitemap files were counted as products
Targets included page.xml.gz and sitemap_1.xml. Control: BHF and M.Video "found" 5 products each.
fixed: exclude .xml / .gz / .txt URLs
.xml.gz archives were never decompressed
The product map read as garbage and the agent found nothing. Control: 0 product nodes despite a non-empty sitemap.
fixed: gzip decompression
Russian storefront URLs do not contain /product/
Sitemap traversal found nothing: most paths look like /smartfony-22899/. Control: Samsung RU was not found at all.
fixed: identifier-in-path heuristic
The parser returned the markup wrapper, not the entity
A node looked like {schema, category, price} — every field empty. Control: name was false for a product with an obvious title.
fixed: node matched by its own @type
Price and availability sat in a nameless Offer node
The entity's own name was ignored and fields read as absent. Control: name = false for a product with a title.
fixed: name from the entity, price and availability from offers
05

Reproduce

Python 3, no dependencies, MIT. Run it against your own catalogue and you get your own numbers, not ours. 

# clone and run against 21 storefronts
git clone https://github.com/Balanov/readiness-probe.git
cd readiness-probe
python readiness_probe.py --targets targets.json --out out

# your own storefront — single target, base mode
python readiness_probe.py --url "https://your-store.example/catalog/" --name YourStore --out out
